FinalYes
Pipeline.php : 36

Collect → write → store: turns a {@see Window} into a stored edition that is ready to be sent for approval.

An edition is a plain array persisted in the StateStore: key, window, status, revision, draft, notes (the facts revisions are checked against), reports, fallback (why the model could not write it, when the draft is the template), history (requested edits), dm_message_ids, posted_message_id, created_at, manual.
